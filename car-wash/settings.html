Settings ​

Every Car Wash setting is edited in /nay, on the Car Wash page (scope nay_car_wash.config). Changes are saved in the database and applied to every player at once. config.lua holds optional starting values: they apply until a setting is changed in /nay, and Reset goes back to them.

The In /nay column is the name shown in the panel. The key is the name used by config.lua and the HTTP API.
