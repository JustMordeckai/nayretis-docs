Settings ​

Every Repair Station setting is edited in /nay, on the Repair Station page (scope nay_repair.config). Changes are saved in the database and applied to every player at once. config.lua holds optional starting values: they apply until a setting is changed in /nay, and Reset goes back to them.

The In /nay column is the name shown in the panel. The key is the name used by config.lua and the HTTP API.

General ​

In /nayKeyTypeDefaultDescription
EnabledEnabledon / offonTurns every station on or off.
Payment accountAccountcash, bank or cash_then_bankcash_then_bankWhere the price is taken from. cash_then_bank uses the bank when cash is short.
Job discountsJobDiscountslist, up to 32noneDiscounts for a job from a grade.
Cooldown (seconds)CooldownSecondsseconds, 0 to 36000Minimum wait between two repairs of the same player. 0 disables it.

Entries of Job discounts:

In /nayKeyTypeDescription

Discount (%)Percentnumber, 1 to 100Discount in percent. 100 makes the repair free.

Services ​

In /nayKeyTypeDefaultDescription
Repair servicesServiceslist, 1 to 8Engine repair $250, Body and tyres $400, Full repair $700Services offered in every station.
Price by damagePriceByDamageon / offonScales the price with the damage of the parts repaired.
Minimum price (%)MinPricePercentnumber, 0 to 10020Lowest share of the service price when pricing by damage.
Refuse undamaged vehiclesRequireDamageon / offonRefuses a service when the parts it repairs are intact.
Blocked vehicle classesBlockedClasseslist of vehicle classescycles, boats, helicopters, planes, trainsVehicle classes refused at the station.

Entries of Repair services:

In /nayKeyTypeDescription
NameLabeltext, up to 32 charactersName shown in the repair menu, the history and webhooks. Optional: left empty, the service is named after the parts it repairs, in the server language (Engine repair, Body and tyres, Full repair...).
PricePricenumber, 0 to 1000000Price at full damage, before discounts and the station percentage.
Duration (seconds)Secondsnumber, 3 to 120How long the repair takes.
Repairs the engineEngineon / offIncludes the fuel tank.
Repairs the bodyBodyon / offIncludes windows, doors and deformation.
Repairs the tyresTyreson / offFixes every burst tyre.

With PriceByDamage on, the price is the service price multiplied by the highest damage of the parts it repairs, never below MinPricePercent and never above 100 percent. Then the station percentage and the job discount apply. With it off, the full price is always charged.

Entries of Blocked vehicle classes have one field, Class (Class), picked from the GTA vehicle classes by name.
